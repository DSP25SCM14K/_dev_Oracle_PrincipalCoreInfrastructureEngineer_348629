to safer systems.

Good infrastructure work closes the loop: see the system clearly, identify the cause, take a bounded action, and prove the recovery.

RELIABILITY CONTROL LOOPSYSTEM NOMINAL

Correlate traces, metrics, and events. Use SLOs to keep routine variation distinct from customer impact.

A

Design for partial failure

Replication, retries, circuit breakers, and explicit partition trade-offs keep a dependency fault from becoming a fleet-wide event.

B

Scale with evidence

Forecast from real utilization. Add headroom where it protects latency, then reclaim it when the demand curve changes.

C

Make operations legible

Instrument the service, test the failure path, and leave the next engineer a clear view of what changed and why.
